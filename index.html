About Us

Frequently Asked Questions

High school tutors are $38/hour online and $40/hour in-person. University tutors are $48/hour online and $50/hour in-person.

In-person tutoring is based in Toronto and Collingwood. Online tutoring is available throughout Canada and the USA.

Our tutors are high school and university honour-roll students. We carefully match students and tutors based on subjects, location, and availability.

In Canada we offer homework help from Grade 1 to Grade 12. In the USA we offer homework help from Grade 6 to Grade 12.

Choose Sign Up for Canada or the USA, complete the form, and our team will contact you to discuss your needs and match you with a tutor.
